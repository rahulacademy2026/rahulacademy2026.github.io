Study notes and chapter guides

Starter summaries to help you revise. Open a subject below.

Curriculum note: These are starter topic guides, not a verified complete 2026 syllabus. Confirm your exact chapter list with the latest CBSE school curriculum and your teacher before exam preparation.
➗ Mathematics — starter guide

Number systems: Learn rational and irrational numbers and how to represent real numbers on a number line.

Algebra: Practise polynomials, identities and factorisation. Show each step clearly and check your answer.

Geometry: Draw labelled figures, state the theorem you use, and give reasons for each step of a proof.

Revision tip: Keep a formula notebook and solve a few problems without looking at worked examples.

🔬 Science — starter guide

Matter: Compare solids, liquids and gases by particle spacing, motion and attraction. Review changes of state.

Motion: Speed = distance ÷ time. For uniform motion, understand distance-time graphs and units.

Living organisms: Review cell structures and the functions of the cell membrane, cytoplasm and nucleus.

Revision tip: Practise labelled diagrams and learn definitions in your own words.

🌍 Social Science — starter guide

History: Build a timeline and connect events with causes and consequences.

Geography: Practise maps, location, climate and how physical features affect people.

Democratic politics: Learn key terms, institutions, rights and responsibilities with examples.

Economics: Understand basic economic terms and use real-life examples to explain them.

📖 English — starter guide

Reading: Read the question first, locate evidence in the passage and answer in your own words.

Grammar: Practise tenses, subject-verb agreement, reported speech and sentence editing as assigned by your school.

Writing: Plan the format, audience and main points before writing. Check spelling and punctuation.

🖋️ Hindi — starter guide

Revise पाठ का सार, लेखक/कवि का परिचय where required, शब्दार्थ and प्रश्न-उत्तर. Practise व्याकरण and लेखन according to your school's prescribed textbook and syllabus.

Revision tip: Write answers in clear sentences and check मात्रा, वर्तनी and punctuation.
