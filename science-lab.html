What is an atom?

An atom is a tiny unit of an element. This simplified model shows a nucleus and electrons around it. The electron paths are for learning and are not exact quantum-mechanical paths.

Remember

The nucleus contains protons and usually neutrons. Electrons occupy regions around the nucleus.

Learning note: these are simplified teaching models, not to scale. Scientific diagrams may simplify shapes and distances to make ideas easier to understand.
