Orienting Yourself: The Use of Coordinates

Welcome to Rahul Academy! Explore step-by-step solutions to selected questions from Class 9 Mathematics Chapter 1.

Question 1: Where do the coordinate axes intersect?

Solution: The x-axis and y-axis intersect at the origin.

Answer: (0, 0)

Question 2: A point W has x-coordinate −5.

A line passes through W parallel to the y-axis. What can we say about a point H on this line?

Solution:

If W = (−5, a), then H = (−5, b), where a and b are y-coordinates.
Answer: H has x-coordinate −5. It lies in Quadrant II or III if its y-coordinate is non-zero. If its y-coordinate is zero, it lies on the negative x-axis.

Question 3: Points R, A, M and P

R = (3, 0), A = (0, −2), M = (−5, −2), P = (−5, 2). Join them in order.

(i) Which sides are perpendicular?

Answer: AM is perpendicular to MP because AM is horizontal and MP is vertical.

(ii) Which side is parallel to an axis?

Answer: AM is parallel to the x-axis. MP is parallel to the y-axis.

(iii) Find two mirror-image points.

Answer: M = (−5, −2) and P = (−5, 2) are mirror images across the x-axis.

Question 4: Construct a right triangle using Z(5, −6)

One possible construction:

Z = (5, −6)
I = (5, 0)
N = (0, −6)

IZ is vertical and ZN is horizontal. Therefore, the angle at Z is 90°.

IZ = 6 units
ZN = 5 units
IN = √(6² + 5²) = √61 units
Answer: One valid right triangle has perpendicular sides of 6 units and 5 units. Other constructions may also be possible.

Question 5: What if negative numbers did not exist?

Without negative coordinates, we could represent points with non-negative x- and y-coordinates, but not every point on the coordinate plane.

Answer: We would not be able to represent points having negative coordinates.

Question 6: Are M, A and G collinear?

M = (−3, −4), A = (0, 0), G = (6, 8)

Slope of AM:

(−4 − 0) / (−3 − 0) = 4/3

Slope of AG:

(8 − 0) / (6 − 0) = 4/3
Answer: Both slopes are equal. Therefore, M, A and G are collinear.

Question 7: Are R, B and C collinear?

R = (−5, −1), B = (−2, −5), C = (4, −12)

Slope of RB:

[−5 − (−1)] / [−2 − (−5)] = −4/3

Slope of BC:

[−12 − (−5)] / [4 − (−2)] = −7/6
Answer: The slopes are unequal. Therefore, the three points are not collinear.

Question 8(i): Right-angled isosceles triangle

Choose these three vertices:

O = (0, 0)
A = (3, 0)
B = (0, 3)

OA = 3 units and OB = 3 units. OA and OB are perpendicular.

Answer: Triangle OAB is a right-angled isosceles triangle.

Question 8(ii): Isosceles triangle in Quadrants III and IV

Choose:

O = (0, 0)
A = (−2, −2) — Quadrant III
B = (2, −2) — Quadrant IV

OA = √8 units and OB = √8 units.

Answer: Since OA = OB, triangle OAB is isosceles.
